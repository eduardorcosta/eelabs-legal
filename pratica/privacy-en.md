# Privacy Policy — Prática

Last updated: 7 November 2026.

Applies to the **Prática** app (iPhone and iPad), published by Eduardo Rocha Costa.

[Versão em português](privacy-pt) · [Support](support-en)

## Summary

- Your data stays **on your device only**.
- The app has no server, no account and no login. There is no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.
- The developer receives no copy of your scores, annotations or practice history, and has no way to access them.
- The subscription is billed by Apple. The developer does not see or store your payment details.

## Data kept on your device

Stored in a local database and in files inside the app, on your device:

- the scores you import (PDF, image, MusicXML and MIDI);
- your annotations, drawing layers and text over the scores;
- the passages and measure regions you mark;
- your exercises, groups, routines, breaks and rhythms;
- your practice history (time practised, days practised and the percentages you declare yourself);
- your preferences (language, accent colour and other settings).

None of this is sent off the device by the app.

## Backup and restore

You pick a folder — for example in the Files app or in iCloud Drive — and Prática copies your data into that folder. That copy is yours and stays where you put it. If you pick an iCloud Drive folder, the copy is then synced by Apple under Apple's terms and privacy policy, not Prática's. The developer receives no copy.

To restore, pick the same folder in the app's restore option.

## Subscription and purchases

Prática is sold by subscription, with a free trial period. Purchase, billing, renewal, cancellation and refunds are handled by **Apple**, through your App Store account. The developer does not receive or store your card number, billing address or any other payment data.

To know your subscription status, the app queries Apple's subscription service from your device. That query carries no data from your scores, annotations or practice history.

To manage or cancel the subscription, use **Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions** on your device, or your App Store account.

## Device permissions

- **Files**: used when you import a score or choose the backup folder. The app only accesses what you pick, at that moment.
- **Notifications**: optional and local, used to tell you an exercise, a break or your practice time for the day has ended. They are not sent by any server.

The app does not request or use camera, microphone, location, photos, contacts, calendar, health or network.

## Children

The app collects no data from anyone, children included.

## Deleting your data

Since nothing leaves the device, there is nothing for the developer to delete. To erase your data, delete the content inside the app or remove the app from the device — removing it erases the local database and the imported files. A backup copy you saved into a folder of your own stays there until you delete it.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the "Last updated" date above changes and the new version is published on this same page.
