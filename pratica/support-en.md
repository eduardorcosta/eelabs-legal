## What Prática is

**Prática** is an app for studying music with your own scores. It has two sides that work together:

- **Scores** — import, prepare and read your material: annotations, drawing layers, measure and passage marking, metronome and audio reference.
- **Practice** — organise your exercises and routines, run the study session with a timer and follow your consistency.

Everything stays on your device. No account, no login, nothing sent off the device.

## Importing a score

1. Open **Scores**.
2. Tap **Import score**.
3. Pick the file: **PDF**, **image**, **MusicXML** (`.musicxml`, `.xml`, `.mxl`) or **MIDI**.
4. Give it a title and confirm. The score appears in your library.

## Annotating and marking measures

Open the score and use the floating palette: drawing, text and layers. Layers are kept apart from the original document, which is always preserved. Under **Measures** you mark regions and passages to study later; under **Focus** the reader shows only the chosen passage.

## Metronome

The metronome is an independent window you move around the screen. You can set BPM, time signature, subdivisions and sound, and keep rhythm variations per score.

## Audio reference

For MusicXML and MIDI scores, **MIDI reference** plays the material so you can check it. It is a study reference, not a recording.

## Practice session and consistency

Build exercises with a duration, group them into groups and routines, add breaks and tap **Start**. The app runs the session with a timer and records your time. Under **Consistency** you see your presence for the week and your history. An exercise counts from **1 minute** practised, and counts **once a day** — the **How does it count?** link at the bottom of Consistency explains the rules.

## Backup and restore

In the app's backup option, pick a folder — for example in the Files app or in iCloud Drive. The app copies your data there. To restore, pick the same folder in the restore option. **Back up before switching devices.** The developer receives no copy of your data.

## Subscription

Prática is sold by subscription, with a free trial period. Billing is handled by Apple, through your App Store account.

To see, manage or cancel: **Settings > [your name] > Subscriptions** on your device. Cancellation and refunds are handled by Apple, not by the developer.

If you have subscribed and the app does not recognise it, tap **Restore purchases** on the subscription screen.

## Languages

Portuguese and English. The app follows your device language.

## Known limitations

- MusicXML scores are engraved by the app and may lay out differently from the editor they were written in.
